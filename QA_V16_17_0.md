1. routine Final Lock → archive VERIFIED → New case;
2. background ≥5 min;
3. background ≥30 min;
4. force close → reopen;
5. offline → reconnect;
6. duplicate-tab/session takeover;
7. update deferred during mutable case;
8. Recovery → handoff → complete → Final Lock;
9. verified backup + test restore;
10. long-case endurance ≥2 h;
11. portrait/landscape + soft keyboard.

Do not interpret the built-in pilot badge as clinical certification; it records device-specific technical acceptance only.
