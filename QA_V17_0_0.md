## Scope

## Required real-device acceptance
Before treating V17 identity/roles as production-ready, test at minimum:
1. Upgrade a populated V16.22 profile and confirm existing staff/PIN migration.
2. Confirm at least one active Administrator remains.
3. Unlock, manual Lock, background auto-lock, and Switch User on Android/Windows/iPad profiles.
4. Confirm Assistant cannot document/void medication administration when Security is enabled.
5. Confirm Nurse/Vet can document medication and that `administeredBy` remains independent from logged-in `documentedBy`.
6. Confirm Case Review and Backup export permissions for Admin/Vet/Nurse.
7. Confirm Restore and Restore rollback require Administrator re-authentication.
8. Confirm Final Sign-off requires Admin/Vet re-authentication and stores actor/session/device metadata.
9. Confirm Hospital Protocol Governance attribution under Admin/Vet accounts.
10. Force-close/reopen during an active case and confirm lock/session behavior does not lose clinical data.
11. Complete Final Lock → Archive VERIFIED → Backup → Verify → Restore in a disposable profile.
12. Repeat the Production Validation protocol and collect actual-device evidence; do not substitute static QA for this step.
