## Limitations
- The validation matrix checks **software arithmetic, unit conversion, range-table integrity, and safety gating**. It does not independently prove that a hospital's selected dose, drug preparation, or protocol is clinically appropriate.
- Full hardware/browser E2E was not completed in this build environment. Continue pilot testing on the actual Android/iPad devices used in the OR.
- No dose, alert threshold, ETT reference table, fluid reference, OR phase logic, Recovery guard, or Final Lock rule was changed in V15.21.0.
