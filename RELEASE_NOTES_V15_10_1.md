# ANESVET V15.10.1 — Pre-OR Briefing Hotfix

## Fixed
- `Reviewed → Open OR LIVE` now passes `skipBriefing` through the OR access gate, preventing the briefing from reopening/blocking navigation after review.

## ETT preparation estimate revised
- Dog normal-anatomy estimate now follows a lean-body-weight preparation table rather than the previous coarse bands.
- Breed/skull-conformation guard added for common brachycephalic dog breeds and Persian/Exotic Shorthair cats, in addition to the existing structured airway risk flags.
- Brachycephalic/BOAS/difficult-airway cases show the weight estimate as preparation context only and widen the tray to include 1–2 smaller sizes.
- Adult feline preparation is anchored to the AAFP 3.5–5.0 mm ID range, with 2.0–5.5 mm availability recommended and BW used only to narrow the starting tray.
- Final ETT size remains clinical/anatomic: use the largest tube that passes the arytenoids easily without trauma.
- Breed is now displayed in the Pre-OR briefing case header.

## Safety
- No ETT estimate is copied to the actual airway record automatically.
- No dose, drug concentration, ASA, alert threshold, or fluid rule was changed.
